Dr. Tariq Aziz

Regional Director South Asia (INI) & Associate Professor of Soil Science

Introduction

Dr. Aziz serves as the Regional Director for South Asia at the International Nitrogen Initiative (INI) and holds the position of Associate Professor of Soil Science at the University of Agriculture, Faisalabad (UAF), Pakistan. He is the lead partner from Pakistan in the global project "South Asia Nitrogen Hub" and is also involved in the UNEP/GEF funded project “International Nitrogen Management Systems (INMS)”.

Dr. Aziz has published more than 120 research articles, and produced >40 M.Sc. and 5 Ph.D students. His research interests include improving fertilizer use efficiency, nutrient recycling, and nutrient pollution.
